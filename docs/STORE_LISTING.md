**Full description:**
PumpWatch is a market-analysis terminal for crypto traders who want to see
the evidence behind every number.

• Meme Radar: trending & new pools on 8 chains, each card shows its data
  source and a 12-point contract safety check (honeypot, tax, LP lock…)
• Whale flow: large-trade pressure per symbol, labeled with the real venue
• Consensus scanner: trend + volume + whale agreement, score explained
• Paper trading: simulated portfolio with journal, R-multiple, drawdown
• Backtest lab: intrabar stops, next-bar fills, real fee & slippage model
• Charts: every candle labeled with its true source; synthesized fallback
  candles are disclosed, never hidden
• Privacy first: non-custodial, no account, encrypted on-device data,
  cloud backup disabled

PumpWatch does NOT hold funds, does NOT place real orders, and does NOT
give personalized financial advice. Signals are statistical observations
with visible evidence and invalidation levels. Crypto assets are volatile;
you can lose money trading them.
